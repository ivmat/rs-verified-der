  the shared iterator (fix: refactor onto that shared walk — a behavioural change, scoped separately).
  Five of seven widened structural harnesses from the 2026-08-23 sweep still carry no `kani::cover` of
  their own, so nothing yet witnesses that their widened loops actually iterate; the covers are owed.
- **R3 (owner decision, 2026-08-25):** whether to wire the new identifier-form-legality check into the
  strict TLV entry point by default, versus keeping it opt-in. Wiring it in touches a Lean-lidded
  function and every existing caller's accepted-input set.

  built on the existing child-walk would extend it to nested structures, and needs its own
  recursion/unwind story.
- **R5 (2026-08-30):** no recursion-depth or total-work bound exists in the decode path itself; the
  crate currently pushes that requirement onto the caller.
- **No Lean lid for the RFC 5280 profile-rule module** — its six harnesses are bounded proofs over
  already-decoded field values, not ∀-length statements over bytes.
- **No CI job for the Lean-lid stage** — scoped (footprint measured at roughly 4.5 GB, not a cost
  blocker), but not yet landed because the job's failing direction has never actually been observed.
- **No `no_std` attribute yet** (the source is `no_std`-ready by measurement) and **final public-API
  review** are both open and explicitly owner-gated, not worked.
